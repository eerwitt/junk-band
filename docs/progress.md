## Deliberately deferred

The user requested mock services and will deploy from a secure shell. No Meta or HF tokens, paid inference, model weights or deployment were used here. SAM Audio dependency/ABI resolution, checkpoint gate, actual load/inference/VRAM metrics and listening evaluation must be established by the supplied live smoke scripts. Research revisions remain candidates until then.

The bundled visual and sound fixtures are generated art and original synthesis, not field recordings. Mock output never demonstrates actual isolation. SAM video tracking, audio visual conditioning, arbitrary skeletal rigs, pitch-preserving stretch and video export are outside this first implementation. Public repository publication and submission video have not been performed.

The software WebGL browser runner reports lower FPS than hardware rendering; no 60 FPS hardware guarantee or inference latency SLA is claimed. Human listening and testing on the actual presentation device are still useful before a judged demo.
