# JUNK — The Living Sampler

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-selected Godot 4 / GDScript for the interface, stage and animation. A small TypeScript browser layer owns native Web Audio, media, IndexedDB and API calls through JavaScriptBridge. Python gateway and Modal GPU workers. React, PixiJS, Zustand and Tone are removed. Modular components and pure timing/animation functions. Build directly from the supplied brief.

## Users

Artists collecting sounds from their own media, and hackathon judges trying a playable creative instrument.

## Product Purpose

Find an object, cut it out, take its sound, and turn it into a dancing musical performer on a circular stage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four bars, 64 steps, deterministic audio-clock scheduling, editable patterns, independent clones sharing immutable assets, media import, explicit candidate review, local persistence and export. Mock services for this implementation; the user will deploy real services later from a secure shell. No local model downloads. No fabricated separation claims. Server secrets never enter browser bundles.

## Brand Commitments

JUNK. Warm eggshell paper, dark ink, burnt orange, mint and yellow. Sunlit circular stage, expressive paper puppets, playful physical instrument. Avoid a traditional DAW. Preserve the supplied plan's visual world.

## Evidence on Hand

AGENT_START_HERE.md and JUNK_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md supplied in Downloads. No original field recordings supplied. Starter sounds must be identified as synthesized demo sounds, and generated imagery as artwork.

## Product Principles

Music works independently of inference. Source seconds differ from composition beats. Users audition and accept model candidates. Honest provenance survives save/export. Accessible controls accompany canvas interactions.
