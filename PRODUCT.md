# JUNK — The Living Sampler

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Godot 4 / GDScript for the interface, stage and animation. A small TypeScript browser layer owns Web Audio, media, IndexedDB and API calls through JavaScriptBridge. Python FastAPI gateway with Meta model APIs, and a Modal GPU worker for SAM Audio.

## Users

Artists collecting sounds from their own media, kids and friends playing with a creative instrument, and hackathon judges trying it cold.

## Product Purpose

Find an object, cut it out, take its sound, and turn it into a musical performer on a circular stage.

## Capabilities and Constraints

A sixteen-bar song in eight sections, deterministic audio-clock scheduling, a base band of CC0 sounds, per-character rhythm cards and pitch-by-height, clones sharing immutable assets, media import with explicit candidate review, local persistence, portable projects and WAV export. Demo mode works with no credentials and labels its stand-in results. No local model downloads, no fabricated separation claims, and server secrets never enter browser bundles.

## Brand Commitments

JUNK. Warm eggshell paper, dark ink, burnt orange, mint and yellow. Sunlit circular stage, expressive cardboard puppets, a playful physical instrument. Never a traditional DAW.

## Product Principles

Music works independently of inference. Source seconds differ from composition beats. Users audition and accept model candidates. Honest provenance survives save and export. Accessible controls accompany canvas interactions.
